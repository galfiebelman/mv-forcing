Real-World Results

We demonstrate generalization of MV-Forcing to real-world data (Sec. B and Fig. 5 in the paper). We replace the synthetic SynCamMaster teacher with a finetuned ReCamMaster and train the view-sequential conditioning path on real-world videos from the Mixkit dataset. Starting from our synthetic-trained model, only 500 finetuning iterations are needed. At inference, Self-Forcing generates the first view from text, and our student autoregressively generates subsequent views. Hover over any video to play.
