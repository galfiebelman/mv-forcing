Long Sequence Comparisons

We compare MV-Forcing against SF+ReCamMaster and SF+ReCamMaster+SF on long multi-view video generation (Tab. 2, Fig. 3, Sec. 4.1 and Fig.7 in the paper). For each scene, we show all 3 views generated by each method. SF+ReCamMaster generates each view as independent 81-frame segments with no shared temporal context, leading to visible degradation beyond the first segment. SF+ReCamMaster+SF recovers temporal continuity but lacks cross-view conditioning, resulting in geometric drift between viewpoints. Our method maintains consistency across both views and time via the accumulated CUT3R geometric prior and spatio-temporal self-forcing. Hover over any video to play.
