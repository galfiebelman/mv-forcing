Short Sequence Comparisons

We compare MV-Forcing against the bidirectional SynCamMaster teacher on short multi-view video generation (Tab. 1, Sec. 4.1 and Fig. 6 in the paper). Despite being a causal few-step student, MV-Forcing achieves comparable visual quality while outperforming the teacher on camera accuracy and cross-view synchronization metrics, demonstrating that explicit geometric grounding through the accumulated CUT3R prior can surpass joint bidirectional denoising even on short sequences.
