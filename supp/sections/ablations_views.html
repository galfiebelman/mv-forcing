View Scaling Ablations

We evaluate how generation quality evolves as the number of views increases from 2 to 5 at a fixed temporal length of 81 frames (Tab. 4, Sec. 4.2 and Fig. 9 in the paper). For each configuration, we show all generated views. The accumulated CUT3R geometric prior grounds each new view in the full 4D structure observed so far, maintaining consistent scene geometry even as the view count grows.
