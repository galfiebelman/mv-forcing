Temporal Scaling Ablations

We evaluate how generation quality evolves as temporal length increases from 81 to 648 frames at a fixed 2 views (Tab. 5, Sec. 4.2 and Fig. 10 in the paper). Cross-view synchronization remains stable across an 8x increase in duration, while visual quality shows slight degradation for long durations. For each configuration, we show both views.
